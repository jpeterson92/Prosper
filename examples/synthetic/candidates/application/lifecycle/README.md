# Source-shaped synthetic advisory lifecycle

T010.1.1 joins the existing [small application fixture](../README.md) through the protected browser/API, actual R1 fit, worksheet, restart, refreshed import and current-format recovery. These files are fictional source observations, not qualified broker exports, observed participant evidence or investor settings.

[initial.csv](initial.csv) and its [reviewed context](initial-context.json) explicitly supply 2.5000 FAAA shares with USD 30.00 total basis and a separately supplied matching position. The outside-account FBBB row retains unknown basis/term and its exclusion/source locator. Missing source coverage and broker display amounts remain unknown. The existing explicit benchmark, marks, history and model settings are reused; sale bounds use the actual checksum/record-derived lot ID. With the USD 10 mark and zero declared costs, selling two shares gives USD 4 price/basis loss, 0.5 remaining shares and USD 20 cash. The browser revises to one share, independently checks USD 2 loss/1.5 shares/USD 10 cash, then saves a dated worksheet.

[refreshed.csv](refreshed.csv) and its [reviewed context](refreshed-context.json) are a second independently stipulated October 26 snapshot: 3.2500 FAAA shares, USD 42.00 total basis and unknown term. They intentionally differ from the original holding and both worksheet proposals. No refreshed fact is computed from intended trades, fills or remaining basis. Selection advances the revision, clears current results and refuses old candidate inputs/new stale worksheet saves; identical worksheet retries still return the original record. Old marks/settings yield a scoped blocked diagnostic rather than a new qualified candidate. Every row remains accounted for.

`frontend/tests/candidates.spec.mjs` owns the integrated case and existing no-qualified/breached-baseline, lost-response, cancellation and recovery cases. `tests/test_advisory_store.py` owns actual concurrent journal churn and deterministic Windows delete-pending/file-alias refusals. Ordinary numerical/validator examples remain in their current pytest modules; no synthetic calibration or larger workload study is added. Ignored `.local/t010.1.1/` holds the actual lifecycle backup, worksheet text, identities and installed-wheel smoke receipt. Historical reads/export/recovery retain original output and both original CSV byte streams without recalculation. Financial/source/model/participant/release acceptance remains separate.
