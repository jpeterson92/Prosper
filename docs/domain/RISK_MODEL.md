## One covariance, scope and horizons

Output ordered daily conditional covariance `H[t+h|t] = D[t+h|t] R[t+h|t] D[t+h|t]`, with cutoff, horizon, parameters, diagnostics and quality state. Validate finite values, symmetry, PSD, security order and required coverage. Use the same identified covariance for baseline/candidate tracking error and group attribution.

With matching annualized covariance `Sigma` and active weights `a = portfolio - benchmark`, `TE = sqrt(a' Sigma a)`. The modeled portfolio is retained stock holdings plus selected additional/residual cash; untouched account cash and excluded holdings stay outside that denominator. Selected USD cash retains its weight under a disclosed zero price-shock approximation, not a yield/FX/rate forecast. The benchmark is 100% retained equities.

Qualify one-session and a disclosed review-horizon projection, initially researching 21 trading sessions. Under the declared constant-weight, zero conditional-mean/cross-day covariance approximation, cumulative active variance is `sum_h a' H[t+h|t] a`; annualized equivalent variance multiplies by `252/horizon`. State the multi-step DCC normalization/expectation approximation. This is not exact compounded buy-and-hold P&L or a guaranteed annual path. Freeze which horizon supplies each displayed limit; one-day annualization is not automatically a forward annual forecast. Final freshness/lookback/horizon/short-history settings require qualification and owner disposition.

## Sector and industry framework

Use sector -> industry -> security mappings for portfolio/benchmark/active weights and enabled absolute/active exposure limits. No taxonomy or investor threshold is invented. Missing classification blocks an applicable constraint unless an explicit reviewed treatment resolves it. Recompute final exposures after whole-share orders and costs using the same sleeve denominator.

For the horizon-matched covariance `C`, `s = sqrt(a' C a)`, security Euler contribution is `a[i] * (C a)[i] / s` for positive TE. Group contributions sum securities and reconcile to TE; negative contributions are valid risk offsets. Zero TE needs defined zero-total/undefined-percentage presentation. Standalone group risk and cross-group covariance are separate diagnostics and are not additive. Sector and nested industry levels are alternative views, not quantities to add together.

This framework explains the stock covariance; it is not an additional factor covariance or causal sector attribution. Factor-residual OHLC is not supplied by the selected source method, so an untested factor/IDR hybrid is not adopted. Separately specified attributable shocks may be scenario diagnostics, never fitted tail probabilities or an extra risk charge. Broad factor/heterotic/asymmetric model searches stay outside v1.
