## Equity-only proxy and weights

Model supported mapped standard equities. Preserve every source row and give unsupported/unmapped holdings explicit exclusion reasons, counts, locators and known excluded values separately from unknown values. ETF units, bonds, derivatives and reserves are outside the modeled portfolio/benchmark. ETF holdings can supply benchmark constituents without making ETF units eligible portfolio holdings. Exclusions do not create modeled or spendable cash.

Let `E` be the sum of original primary USD market values for retained supported mapped equities, counting each retained economic row once. Derive Float64 weights as `marketValue / E`; the derived retained weights sum approximately to one under [ADVISORY_NUMERICS](../engineering/ADVISORY_NUMERICS.md). Preserve published percentages separately, including blanks, rounded totals and contradictions. Do not normalize rounded published percentages or fabricate zeros. The superseded all-primary equity/reserve/derivative denominator is not the modeled denominator; no old diagnostic implementation must be preserved.

Block the affected benchmark result for unqualified fund identity, incomplete/ambiguous row accounting, contradictory included mappings, missing/nonfinite/negative required included values or nonpositive E. Explicitly excluded unsupported instruments do not require derivative models or their own risk histories. Exclusion cannot hide damage affecting retained facts. A mapped equity still needs its separate tax/candidate eligibility and required risk coverage.

Label the result an **equity-only disclosed-holdings proxy**. It is neither an exact index feed nor full-fund/ETF NAV-return replication. No source weight difference can silently become cash or a financial-readiness claim.

An observed zero included value is distinct from unknown. Zero-weight consumer/history treatment still needs explicit qualification; do not silently delete retained rows to bypass coverage or numerical requirements.

### Total-return drift to a later effective date

Owner instruction October 27 adopts total-return drift when disclosed benchmark holdings are used for a later analysis date. Preserve the source-date values/weights and reported date. For retained source weights `b_i(s)` and compatible constituent simple total returns over declared sessions `s < d <= t`, derive `g_i = product(1 + r_i,d)` and `b_i(t) = b_i(s) * g_i / sum_j(b_j(s) * g_j)`. Reinvest constituent distributions through the supplied total-return convention; do not add dividends a second time. This is a fixed-constituent, reinvested equity-only disclosed-holdings proxy. It does not reconstruct fund trades, additions/deletions, rebalances, cash management, fees or actual later holdings.

`benchmark_drift.weights_at` is the shared application calculation. Modeled-input members use the later-date weights; candidate preparation checks them against the same frozen dated inputs before fitting, and candidate/worksheet metrics consume that vector. Preserve source/target dates and the reinvestment/proxy assumption in saved warnings, including candidate/worksheet exports. Historical saved outputs remain dated records and are never recalculated on read.

Require sourced compatible USD daily total returns, coherent vintages and the complete declared session grid for every retained benchmark name, including zero-weight members. The source date must be on or after the history predecessor; all returns after the source date through the target session must be covered. Exclude the source-day return. The declared calendar covers intervening nontrading dates; never invent a flat tail beyond the supplied cutoff. The current implementation requires the target to equal the declared history cutoff for forward drift. Missing/invalid returns, future holdings, incompatible conventions/vintages or unrepresentable compounded values make affected weights and suggestions unavailable. Do not silently retain old weights, omit names or infer zero returns. An input window that starts after the holdings date must be extended explicitly.

Drift does not reset the original holdings age or waive the reviewed maximum-age policy. Same-date weights remain the reported-date weights. Low-level basket estimates require weights dated to their valuation date and refuse an old weight even if its source assertion has a longer validity window. Actual investor lots/positions continue to use supplied quantities and dated marks for valuation; total-return proxy reinvestment does not create account shares, dividends/cash, transactions or tax basis. Unknown account activity still requires a separately refreshed snapshot.
