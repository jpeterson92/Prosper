### Proposed research calculation

This is an experiment proposal within the accepted shrinkage direction, not adoption of D012/D017 settings or implementation of actionable risk controls:

1. Build the full supported union of held, benchmark and candidate securities. Use a common USD close/calendar and an explicitly frozen as-of date. Compare illustrative two-, three- and five-year daily windows rather than treating one lookback as approved. The supplied total-market workbook is not silently truncated to the fictional 500-name fixture; missing constituents or basket changes require explicit disposition.
2. Compute simple research returns `r[i,t] = adjusted_close[i,t] / adjusted_close[i,t-1] - 1`. Do not add dividends again to that adjusted-price return. The vendor adjustment convention is an approximation to the desired reinvested-return convention and must be checked around dividends, splits and exceptional events. Adjusted history never supplies tax basis or a review-time execution quote.
3. Estimate daily covariance using [scikit-learn's LedoitWolf reference](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.covariance.LedoitWolf.html), which shrinks sample covariance toward a scaled identity. This stabilizes a noisy large matrix; it does not establish forecast accuracy. Compare against simple sample/diagonal baselines and held-out realized active returns. Any new library remains subject to normal dependency/version/license qualification.
4. For active weights `a = portfolio_weights - benchmark_weights` on identical securities and scope, estimate `TE = sqrt(252 * a.T * daily_covariance * a)`. Analogously use portfolio weights for volatility. The 252-day scaling is an explicit research convention, not adopted freshness/annualization policy. Weights retain the stock-sleeve-plus-selected-cash denominator; zero-risk cash would be a disclosed simplifying assumption requiring consistent benchmark treatment.
5. Retain missing/stale/short histories and identity/action anomalies. Do not forward-fill prices into invented zero returns, silently drop holdings, normalize away missing benchmark weight or infer zero risk. Use a common valid observation panel for the bounded reference; an unsupported name receives a visible unavailable result until a separately approved fallback is qualified. Assess PSD/conditioning, coverage and sensitivity before any optimizer use.

A later factor experiment can estimate stock exposures to market/sector or French factors and form `Sigma = B F B.T + D`, retaining stock-specific residual variance. It is not required for the first shrinkage prototype. No expected-return forecast or LLM is needed for covariance/active-risk calculations. Current-name history can support forward-looking research, but does not prove a survivorship-free historical strategy backtest.
