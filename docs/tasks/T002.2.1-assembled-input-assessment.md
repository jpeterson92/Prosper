## Interface and meaning

`prosper.assembly.assess_inputs(inspection, supplements, fictional=True)` takes the existing in-process `LotInspection` plus frozen `Supplements`, and returns an immutable `Assessment`. This is a Python library interface; it opens no files, installs no dependencies, changes no runtime state and exposes no HTTP/UI route. Caller-declared fictional input is required and is not a detector of real data. `LotInspection` is trusted inspector output, not an untrusted serialization format; structural inconsistencies are refused as findings, but this boundary cannot authenticate original bytes or a deliberately forged projection.

`Evidence` retains fictional actor, source reference, effective time and recorded time. Both times require explicit timezones and remain separate. `AccountScope` explicitly binds each source account to a profile/account and traded/monitor/excluded role; one traded account and one profile are required for assembly sufficiency. Every observed lot retains its original `LotObservation`, source cells/locators/exact values, account assertion and record-specific `SecurityLink`. Links and stock-scope assertions are unqualified declarations, not asset classification, household coverage or durable sale-lot qualification.

Opening assertions explicitly supply USD currency, economic cutoff, snapshot inclusion, stock scope and the disclosed source-reported-total-basis ordinary-opening assumption. Fixed assertion tokens are documented by the `expected` mapping in the implementation; no source label, dollar sign, collection time, average basis or term text supplies these facts implicitly. Cutoff assertion value and its effective time must agree. Duplicate/conflicting bindings, missing identity/quantity, unknown required traded-lot basis, incomplete/invalid observations and unsupported assertions remain located assembly findings. Unknown term alone is not an assembly failure. There is no event replay, basis adjustment, loss calculation or tax classification.

Three aggregate fields cover **all source observations**, including monitoring/excluded accounts: quantity, reported total basis and source current value. They are not independently reconciled positions, current quotes or sleeve/account NAV. Unknowns propagate and exact arithmetic overflow produces an explicit unknown and assembly failure. Identical-looking lots remain distinct. No sleeve diagnostic is emitted, so A07's optional stipulated-NAV calculation is outside this interface.

Imported cash, asserted usable cash and pending commitments are separate account/currency-scoped `CashFact` tuples. An optional `CashSelection` preserves exact amount, evidence and review state. Unset selection projects zero with `unset_default_requires_review`; explicit unreviewed/reviewed zero remain distinct. Different amounts with the same account/currency/effective time are contradictory; distinct effective times retain a timing gap. Selection above an attributed usable amount is compared only when their effective times match. Wrong scope/currency also exposes a funding gap. These are diagnostic time comparisons, not a selected freshness horizon or proof of current buying power. Commitments retain their amount and evidence; they are never silently subtracted from already asserted usable funds, released or interpreted as a proven shortfall. Missing imported cash stays absent/unknown; a zero budget does not assert a zero account balance. No trade funding equation is implemented.

History claims, manual names, resolved references and supplied known conflicts remain distinct and ordered. Empty manual lists and claims of complete history never clear the unverified-wash-sale warning. Unresolved names remain unresolved. Restriction intent is both sale and replacement-buy legs; T005 still owns actual identity-relation/date/conflict screening. Manual execution assertions remain unqualified evidence, with confirmation/allocation/reconciliation gaps owned by later workflow stages.

Findings have separate assembly, wash, funding, execution and future-dependency scopes. `assembly_sufficient` describes only this declared-input boundary. Read-only properties always return `planning_ready=False`, `canonical_checkpoint_ready=False` and `wash_verification='unverified'`. Missing quote/benchmark/risk/constraint, source/tax and sale-lot qualification remain explicit. No financial-readiness or clean-wash state exists here.

Supplement text is capped at 256 characters, collections at 10,000 entries each and findings at 100. Oversized/mutable/wrong-typed supplements raise safe fixed-location `AssemblyError` without input values. Findings overflow is explicit and prevents assembly sufficiency. Input evidence remains retained in full within accepted bounds. Sensitive dataclasses omit field values from representations; result findings expose fixed codes and numeric record locations only. `input_sha256` includes version, complete retained observation and all supplements, original exact spellings, times and tuple order; it is deterministic local input identity, not persistent generation or correction authority.
