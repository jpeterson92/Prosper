## Covariance checks and interpretation

Seven synthetic case groups pass:

1. A four-observation, two-security rational oracle yields centered empirical covariance `[[1/2000, 1/10000], [1/10000, 9/20000]]`. Scalar `Fraction` moments independently compute the shrinkage coefficient/covariance without calling NumPy/sklearn covariance routines. The existing V12 annual covariance/active-weight example yields `sqrt(0.0013)`, about 3.60555% tracking error.
2. A deterministic fictional 240-by-six correlated panel exercises an interior shrinkage coefficient (about 0.00447413). Rational scalar outer-product deviations agree with sklearn; PSD, security-order permutation, zero active weights and proportional active-weight scaling pass.
3. Missing returns are refused without fill/drop.
4. Infinite returns are refused.
5. Constant security histories are refused rather than interpreted as proven zero risk.
6. Short panels are refused under this experiment's explicit thirty-observation guard.
7. One-security panels are refused under this bounded multi-security experiment.

The guard is experimental scaffolding, not an adopted lookback, identity/coverage fallback or production policy. It does not qualify all possible malformed/rank-deficient datasets. The oracle is independently formulated arithmetic reviewed by this same agent, not independent specialist review. No agent-written check establishes financial correctness or forecasting suitability.

The public panel uses the first **1,005 observations for training** and final **252 for held-out observation**. Compare mean-centered empirical covariance with denominator `n`, its diagonal baseline, and sklearn Ledoit-Wolf toward `trace(S)/p * I`. The observed shrinkage coefficient is about **0.02067845**; condition number decreases from about **21.59 to 19.27** and the smallest eigenvalue is positive. This supports numerical usability in this panel, not a required magnitude of improvement.

For one explicitly fictional active-weight vector, the sample estimate is closer to held-out realized risk than the shrinkage estimate; the diagonal baseline is farther away. Therefore **this experiment does not demonstrate forecasting superiority for Ledoit-Wolf**. The exact comparison, weights and arrays remain local in `market-results.json`, avoiding publication of vendor-derived market arrays. Research assumptions are simple adjusted-price returns, 252-day variance scaling, identical aligned securities, no selected cash and no expected-return forecast. None selects product D012/D017 settings or validates a stock-sleeve/cash/benchmark pipeline. No actual benchmark file is read.
