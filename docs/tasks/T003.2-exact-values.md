### Numeric semantics and review findings

- `ExactValue` validates fixed-point ASCII strings with an explicit `Sign` rule and field location. Exponents, nonfinite spellings, whitespace, plus signs, float/int/bool/Decimal inputs and unsupported precision fail explicitly. Source spelling and decimal precision unit survive canonicalization; signed zero canonicalizes to `0`, while nonnegative/positive fields reject negative spelling, including `-0`.
- Source bounds count coefficient digits after leading zeros, including significant trailing zeros, before normalization, with at most 12 source fractional digits. Redundant zeros within the admitted bounds are preserved; excess source precision is rejected. A 42-character pre-parse bound also rejects excessive zero padding. These are engineering capability bounds, not financial defaults.
- `parse_value` accepts explicit null only with a nonempty reason and rejects a reason paired with a known value. Unknown arithmetic combines reasons in stable order without inferring zero, including unknown times zero. The allocation keeps known residual quantity while propagating missing basis/quantum into both basis outputs; known invalid facts still fail.
- Addition, subtraction and multiplication use a fresh precision-80 Decimal context with explicit rounding, exponent limits and traps, independent of ambient context. They reject outputs outside the book bounds rather than rounding to fit. Intermediate products may exceed the 28-digit book bound when the final diagnostic allocation remains supported. No binary float is accepted in authoritative arithmetic. There is no general rounded-division API.
- Diagnostic allocation requires positive known remaining/sold quantities, sold no greater than remaining, nonnegative basis, and an explicit known power-of-ten quantum aligned with basis. Source precision unit and separately supplied basis quantum are distinct: imported basis `181` can use declared quantum `0.01`. Unsupported quantum such as `0.05`, mismatched basis or unknown quantum cannot silently become a qualified allocation. This first synthetic unit support does not claim all broker conventions.
- Partial allocation rounds the pro-rata result HALF_EVEN at that quantum, then subtracts it from the unrounded remaining total. Full disposal consumes the entire remaining basis. Unsupported residual quantity/basis fails rather than truncating. Derived output spellings describe results; retain original input objects and explicit allocation quantum for source evidence. Compare known numeric amounts through `.value`; object equality also retains source spelling/sign metadata.
