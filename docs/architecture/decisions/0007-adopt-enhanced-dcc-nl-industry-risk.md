# ADR-0007: Enhanced DCC-NL with sector and industry risk

Date: October 23 PDT / October 24, 2026 UTC. Status: **Accepted methodology direction; implementation, settings and financial qualification pending**. Decision: D041. Approver: project owner, explicit direction to update docs/plans to use the best supported DCC-NL version with helpful enhancements and a sector/industry risk framework.

## Decision and scope

Adopt **enhanced DCC-NL as Prosper v1's covariance/forward-tracking-error methodology**, with **IDR-DCC-NL using qualified daily OHLC inputs as the production target**, and an integrated hierarchical sector/industry exposure, limit and risk-attribution framework. Use one identified total covariance forecast for portfolio risk, benchmark-relative risk and group attribution. The previous linear-shrinkage-correlation/EWMA-volatility suggestion is superseded; it may remain a transparent validation reference, not the selected production model.

"Best" means the best supported target identified for these inputs and this use, conditional on observed quality/calibration. It is not a universal performance guarantee or authority to combine every enhancement. Broad model-family comparisons stay in [v2 research](../../tasks/V2-risk-model-comparison.md). The v1 work includes faithful model qualification, standard DCC-NL reference parity, forward TE calibration and necessary independent review; no model is implemented by this decision.

## Evidence and selected enhancements

- [Engle, Ledoit and Wolf (2019)](https://www.econ.uzh.ch/dam/jcr%3A28fa9939-753e-4f5c-932d-945872f30cfd/jbes_2019.pdf) supplies the DCC-NL reference: fitted individual volatility, devolatilized innovations, nonlinear-shrunk correlation target and composite-likelihood correlation dynamics.
- [De Nard, Engle, Ledoit and Wolf (2022)](https://www.econ.uzh.ch/dam/jcr%3A5ee13ed0-14df-4562-9beb-086a2d76738f/jbf_2022.pdf) supplies the enhanced target: OHLC-based volatility information and consistently regularized returns for variance/correlation estimation (IDR-DCC-NL, section 3 and Appendix A). Its implementation uses analytical nonlinear shrinkage from Ledoit-Wolf (2020). These are daily OHLC summaries, not a required historical intraday tape.
- [De Nard, Ledoit and Wolf (2025)](https://www.econ.uzh.ch/dam/jcr%3A8f7e20cf-e9a7-4fde-87cb-aef82de26404/jpm_2025.pdf) supplies directly relevant evidence for DCC-NL on benchmark-relative management. It does not establish that the OHLC enhancement, industry framework or particular Prosper settings improve TE until tested here.

The 2022 paper specifically explains why its OHLC approach cannot simply be stacked on a factor-residual DCC model: residual OHLC observations are unavailable. Consequently the v1 industry framework below groups/explains the chosen stock covariance; it is not an independently added factor covariance. Factor-augmented/heterotic alternatives remain v2 candidates. GJR/asymmetric dynamics, different shrinkage losses or a custom combination are not silently selected as further improvements.

## Model contract

1. **Qualified frozen inputs.** Use the complete supported held/benchmark/candidate union, resolved security identities and dated return/OHLC/action evidence through [D040's persistent cache](0006-adopt-persistent-market-history-cache.md). Keep original bytes/lexemes, provider identity, adjustment vintages, timezone/session/calendar and transform versions. Validate positive compatible prices and OHLC ordering; explicitly resolve overnight/corporate-action alignment and the relation between source total returns, synthetic preceding close and volatility proxies. Do not mix adjusted close with incompatible OHLC, add dividends twice, fill missing returns with zero or infer sector identity from a ticker. Current prices remain distinct execution/reference evidence.
2. **Published numerical specification.** Pin the published equations, units, software/reference version and convergence tolerances before implementation. Qualify the GARCH/DCC stationarity/positivity constraints, initialization, likelihood, parameter fitting, forecast normalization and analytical nonlinear-shrinkage behavior including dimension above sample size. Reference faithful GARCH(1,1)/DCC-NL first; qualify the selected IDR regularized-return construction consistently in both stages. The intended OHLC proxy is the paper's overnight-inclusive full-OHLC specification (equation 4.7), subject to source/units checks. Parameters such as proxy constants and regularization scaling are qualified model settings, not arbitrary investor limits. No new dependency/license or numerical default is selected by naming an algorithm.
3. **One forecast and explicit horizon.** Output ordered daily conditional covariance `H[t+h|t] = D[t+h|t] R[t+h|t] D[t+h|t]` with cutoff, horizon, fit diagnostics, parameters and quality state. Qualify one-day and a disclosed review-horizon projection (initial research target: 21 trading sessions), including the approximation used for multi-step DCC normalization/expectations. For constant active weights and the declared zero conditional mean/cross-day covariance approximation, cumulative active variance is `sum_h a' H[t+h|t] a`; equivalent annualized risk uses `252/horizon` times that variance. Label this fixed-weight approximation, not exact compounded buy-and-hold P&L or a guaranteed one-year forecast. Freeze which horizon supplies each displayed limit. D017 retains final freshness/horizon calibration; do not silently equate one-day annualization with a forward annual path.
4. **Scope and validation.** `a = portfolio_weights - benchmark_weights` uses the stock sleeve plus selected cash under D039. A disclosed zero daily price-shock approximation for selected/residual USD cash preserves its weight and consistent benchmark treatment; it supplies no cash yield, FX or rate forecast. Validate symmetry, finite values, PSD, security order, convergence and full coverage. A valid covariance is not evidence of calibrated TE; optimizer-selected portfolios and signed active positions require forward testing.
5. **Failure and variant selection.** Bad/missing OHLC cannot masquerade as a qualified enhanced model. Standard returns-only DCC-NL is the required named reference and a possible separately qualified supported variant; production use requires a recorded model/coverage/calibration disposition, visible variant and frozen settings, not an automatic downgrade, per-row proxy switch or hidden mixture. If the enhancement is not qualified, retain the gap and choose the supported variant through an explicit evidence-backed disposition. A failed fit/stale input remains unavailable or diagnostic-only; no automatic return to the superseded EWMA model. Different variants/settings produce different model versions and invalidate dependent unexecuted results.

## Integrated sector and industry framework

Adopt a versioned hierarchy of **sector -> industry -> security**, using a qualified permitted source or attributable reviewed mapping. Freeze source/taxonomy name, version/as-of, security mapping, effective versus recorded time, manual corrections and unknowns with each run. Do not label a provider taxonomy GICS/BICS without evidence or treat current classifications as point-in-time historical classifications.

V1 includes:

- Portfolio, benchmark and active weights by sector and industry, using the same modeled sleeve denominator and declared cash treatment. Show absolute concentration and benchmark-relative over/underweights.
- User-selected enabled/disabled absolute and active exposure limits at sector/industry level. Numerical limits are explicit profile inputs; no invented thresholds or automatic relaxation. Independent final-state validation recomputes them after whole-share rounding/costs. Missing classification blocks an applicable constrained result unless an explicit reviewed treatment resolves that scope; do not silently exclude the security or treat its exposure as zero.
- **Signed risk contributions from the same covariance.** At a chosen forecast horizon, let `C` be the matching daily/cumulative/annualized covariance, `a` the active weights and `s = sqrt(a' C a)`. Security contribution is `a[i] * (C a)[i] / s` when `s > 0`; summing securities in group `g` gives that group's Euler contribution. All disjoint groups sum to `s`. Contributions may be negative because positions hedge other risks. Zero TE has a defined zero-total/undefined-percentage presentation, never division by zero.
- Standalone group risk and cross-group covariance effects are separate diagnostics. Standalone risks are not additive; sector and industry levels are alternative nested views, not quantities to add together. A sector's Euler contribution is not a pure estimated sector-factor exposure or causal attribution. Do not add another industry covariance to `C`, erase observed within-group relationships or replace TE with sector similarity.
- Attributable, explicitly stipulated sector/industry shock scenarios may complement the model where separately specified. They are scenario P&L diagnostics, not fitted covariance, tail probabilities or an extra risk charge automatically added to TE.

This supplies a coherent sector/industry risk framework in v1 without claiming an untested factor/OHLC hybrid. Country/currency expansion stays outside the current ordinary-domestic-equity baseline.

## Alternatives, remaining decisions and reconsideration

Superseded production suggestion: historical linear-shrinkage correlation plus EWMA volatility. Retain it only as a transparent reference. Broad nonlinear/factor/heterotic/OHLC alternative searches remain deferred to v2; targeted implementation/parity/calibration of the selected enhancement belongs in v1. Open D012/D017 retain provider/implementation/settings, freshness, short-history policy, classification details, supported scale and investor limits. Source rights, independent model review, G1-G3, distribution and financial-use acceptance remain separate.

Reconsider the enhancement or selected supported variant if source quality/permission, numerical fitting, projected TE calibration, actual universe scale, classification burden or operational evidence fails the stated acceptance. Record a new disposition or superseding decision; do not relabel a convenient fallback "best." This adoption changes documentation only: no application, fixture, executable test, dependency, market-data acquisition, private-reference access, Git mutation or release is included.
