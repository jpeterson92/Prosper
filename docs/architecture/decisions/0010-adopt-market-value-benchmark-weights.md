# ADR-0010: Derive holdings-proxy weights from complete source market values

Date: October 25, 2026 PDT. Status: **Accepted by the project owner**, who replied **I approve all the proposed** to the [T006.1.8.2 proposal](../../tasks/T006.1.8.2-source-qualification.md#weight-basis-proposal-and-subsequent-owner-adoption). Implements D044 and amends ADR-0009's previously unselected weight-derivation policy. This is policy adoption; implementation and real benchmark qualification remain open.

**October 25 scope amendment:** [D045/ADR-0011](0011-adopt-equity-only-scope-and-final-cash-target.md) now selects an equity-only modeled sleeve/benchmark, explicit unsupported/unmapped exclusions, exact retained-equity-value weights summing to one and a separate optional final cash target. It supersedes conflicting all-primary inclusion/denominator requirements below while preserving complete original source evidence and applicable tax/conflict/funding rules. The earlier decision text remains historical evidence; runtime implementation is separate.

## Question, context and evidence

How should Prosper derive exact allocation weights for the already selected disclosed full-portfolio holdings proxy when published percentages are rounded or blank? [The economic assessment](../../tasks/T006.1.8.2-source-qualification.md#october-25-economic-follow-up-and-owner-decision) retains a complete diagnostic ledger across four byte-identified VTI/VOO snapshots. All 7,952 numeric percentages are compatible with nearest-five-decimal percentage-point rounding against the combined primary market-value total; 185 blank percentages have small positive values. This is observed arithmetic, not a provider rounding guarantee. June issuer statements also distinguish that total from whole-fund and ETF-class NAV.

## Decision and consequences

- For this holdings-allocation proxy, define `T` as the exact sum of original `marketValue` over every primary equity, reserve and derivative row, counting each economic source row once after complete qualified representation reconciliation. Derive each allocation as the exact rational `marketValue / T`. Preserve source decimal lexemes and the original published percentage separately, including blanks and contradictions.
- Count all primary rows and retain signed weights. A blank published percentage may have a positive derived allocation when its source value and denominator are qualified; the source percentage remains unknown. Never normalize the partial sum of rounded percentages, infer source zero, clip negatives, omit unsupported holdings or fabricate residual cash.
- Refuse derivation/qualification for missing or nonfinite required values, nonpositive T, unqualified source identity or incomplete/ambiguous representation reconciliation. Preserve all original arrays, rows, unknown fields and locators even when qualified duplicate/projection representations are excluded from the economic sum. Observed relationships must be revalidated for each frozen source version.
- Keep allocation derivation separate from qualified instrument/cash/financing and return treatment. Unsupported or conflicting rows remain blockers for dependent risk/activation. This decision adopts no reserve-to-cash conversion, derivative-to-stock/index mapping, notional/fair-value interpretation, residual treatment or resolution of the documented contract-date conflicts.
- Describe the result as a disclosed-holdings allocation proxy. It is not an exact index or a claim to reproduce ETF NAV returns. No ETF or date is preselected. Existing source permission, immutable review/version behavior, no basket/fallback policy and secondary custom-file route remain in force.

## Alternatives and acceptance evidence

The reviewed source-only alternative would await complete issuer numeric weights and economic definitions. The owner selected the proposed market-value derivation. A fund-NAV-return target would require a separate accounting/financing contract; it was not the proposed holdings-proxy decision. Rounded-percentage rescaling, zero-filling and equity-only omission remain unsupported.

The approval accepts the proposed weight basis, preservation/refusal rules and remaining qualification boundaries. It does not implement a transformed basket or accept a real benchmark. The existing long-only synthetic basket does not acquire signed-reserve/derivative support through this document. A later authorized implementation must retain exact source and derived identities, prove complete row/representation coverage and signed/unknown/failure behavior, and qualify its consumers. Real security/currency/history/taxonomy/freshness/scale, instrument/financing treatment, independent financial/model review and G1-G3 remain open. No broker, publication, deployment, release or Git action is authorized by this approval.

Reconsider on changed source/schema/representation semantics, inconsistent value units or economic meaning, unavailable complete values, a changed benchmark objective, independent review findings or explicit owner direction. Do not silently switch denominator or repair the retained source to preserve readiness.
