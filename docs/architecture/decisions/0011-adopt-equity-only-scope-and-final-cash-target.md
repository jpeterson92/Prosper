# ADR-0011: Adopt an equity-only modeled sleeve and benchmark with a selectable final cash balance

Date: October 25, 2026

Status: **Accepted by the project owner** in the ongoing T006.1.8.2 qualification task. The owner directed excluding holdings that cannot be mapped to standard equity line items, deferring ETFs, bonds and other securities, and allowing a selected final cash balance for raising or reinvesting cash. The explicit follow-up **Yes - rescale retained equities to 100%** adopts the benchmark denominator below. Recorded as D045; amends D011, D039 and D044. Adoption established requirements; the implementation follow-up below now records local synthetic evidence, with real use and financial acceptance still gated.

## Decision and scope

- Model an existing long-only equity sleeve plus explicitly selected portfolio cash. Include only holdings mapped with attributable evidence to supported standard equity line items. Exclude unsupported instruments and unresolved mappings from the modeled sleeve/benchmark for now, retaining every original source row, quantity, value, identifier, unknown and reason in a complete inclusion/exclusion ledger. An unresolved row remains unresolved in the source; exclusion does not fabricate its identity or classification. ETFs may supply full holdings as the benchmark source without becoming eligible portfolio holdings. ETF units as modeled holdings, bonds, derivatives, reserve instruments and other unsupported securities remain deferred.
- For the standard Vanguard benchmark, acquire the complete original portfolio and reconcile alternate/duplicate representations, then select the supported mapped equity rows. Define `E` as the exact sum of their original primary `marketValue` values, counting each retained economic row once. Derive each benchmark weight as the exact rational `marketValue / E`. The retained equities sum to one. This explicitly replaces D044's all-primary equity/reserve/derivative denominator for the modeled benchmark; retain the old all-primary diagnostic and original published percentages as separate source evidence when available. Do not normalize rounded published percentages or invent weights for excluded rows.
- Show the selection policy/version, retained and excluded counts, source locators, exclusion reasons and known excluded source values separately from their unknowns. Exclusion changes the benchmark's scope: label it an **equity-only disclosed-holdings proxy**, with no ETF NAV-return or full-portfolio replication claim. Excluded values do not become portfolio cash or a benchmark cash allocation. No fund/date is preselected and no creation/redemption basket or source/date fallback is permitted.
- Preserve complete source/representation proof and refuse unqualified source identity, incomplete/ambiguous row accounting, conflicting included mappings, missing/nonfinite required included values, negative included values or nonpositive E. An unresolved/unsupported row with an explicit exclusion disposition does not by itself require its risk history or derivative/financing model. Required identity, USD units, supported equity classification, market history and taxonomy coverage remain complete for the retained modeled union. An explicitly observed zero included value is distinct from an unknown; existing consumer zero-weight rules still require qualification. Exclusion cannot conceal corruption or contradictions affecting the retained rows or denominator.
- Add an optional, explicit **final cash balance target** on the portfolio side, separate from imported account cash and the initial `cash_to_invest` funding budget. Support raising cash by leaving net eligible sale proceeds uninvested or reinvesting selected available cash, subject to the same gain, risk, costs, funding, reservation and whole-share constraints. Cash stays in the final sleeve NAV and risk calculation; the benchmark remains 100% retained equity. Untouched account cash stays outside the sleeve. Do not infer funding or use excluded source values as spendable cash.
- Record whether the target is enabled, its exact amount/currency and any explicitly reviewed tolerance/constraint treatment before generation; freeze these with the run. No implicit target, full-balance spending instruction, tolerance or cap relaxation is adopted. Whole-share/cost constraints may prevent an exact target: report the mismatch/no-qualified-candidate result rather than silently changing the target. Raising cash does not authorize positive-gain sales or a new tax objective. Detailed investor settings and solver/UI qualification remain with D012/T007/T008.

D034 tax eligibility and D038 household/recent-name restrictions remain separate from equity risk representation. A mapped equity is not automatically a qualified harvest sale or replacement. Retain excluded observations for applicable restrictions, tax and reconciliation diagnostics; scope omission cannot clear a known wash-sale conflict or an unbounded dependency affecting included holdings. Label sleeve results accurately instead of claiming account/household completeness. No real account or portfolio processing is authorized by this decision.

## Hand-derived acceptance requirements

| Case | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| Fictional primary equity values 60 and 30, excluded reserve/derivative values totaling 10 | E = 90; retained benchmark weights exactly 2/3 and 1/3. All source rows remain; excluded 10 is not modeled cash. This replaces the prior full-source allocations 3/5 and 3/10. |
| A retained equity has a blank source percentage but a qualified value and identity | Original percentage remains unknown; derive its weight from value/E, without source zero-filling. |
| An unmapped row, an ETF and a bond are present; an alternate array repeats retained rows | Explicit exclusion reasons for the first three; complete source/alternate links and no double count. Missing identities/histories of excluded rows do not become fictitious modeled equities. |
| No retained equity, nonpositive E, damaged source/row links or a contradictory included identity/value | Block qualified benchmark construction, preserving all evidence. No empty/previous benchmark fallback. |
| Synthetic portfolio starts with stock value 100 and selected cash 0; target final cash 20; eligible sales net 20 and no buys | Final stock 80/cash 20/NAV 100 before any other value changes; compare equity weights 0.8 and cash 0.2 against the equity-only benchmark, not a benchmark rescaled to match cash. |
| Synthetic portfolio starts with stock 80/selected cash 20; eligible buys spend 15, no fees or other changes | Final stock 95/cash 5/NAV 100. Imported/untouched account cash remains separate. A different final cash target or funding budget creates a new run. |
| Target requires a prohibited gain sale, unavailable funding or an unattainable whole-share amount | Preserve the target and constraints; report no qualified candidate or the explicitly evaluated residual. Do not force a trade or silently relax. |

These are independently stated future acceptance cases, not executed application/model tests. Historical synthetic .1/.8.1 contracts and prior research artifacts retain their original scope until an authorized adaptation is implemented and verified.
