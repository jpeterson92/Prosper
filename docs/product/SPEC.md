# Product specification

Current v1 requirements, October 25, 2026. [STATUS](../STATUS.md) records implementation evidence; [delivery plan](../PROJECT_PLAN.md) and [remaining tasks](../tasks/REMAINING_WORK_PLAN.md) own execution. ## Current MVP milestone

The owner adopted a personal local research MVP on October 27. Deliver useful actual-input diagnostics first, then exploratory use of the existing model/candidate/worksheet path, then observed improvements. Mathematical input sufficiency and all enabled hard limits remain mandatory. Independent review/calibration gates govern financial reliance and accepted quality claims, rather than basic import or clearly labeled exploration. Public installer/support/release work is later. One local workspace and one set of input/calculation contracts serve every input; the app has no synthetic-versus-real category. The [delivery route](../tasks/REMAINING_WORK_PLAN.md#mvp-delivery-route) owns sequencing.

Owner first-use direction: the average investor should **upload personal holdings, select a benchmark and receive a basic TLH proposal**. Preset routine preparation/settings as far as possible, use end-of-day prices and one standard risk preset, and place expert overrides behind collapsed **Advanced settings**. Populate attributable source facts, supported mappings, benchmark-stock replacement eligibility and policy-based exclusions automatically where the evidence/rules suffice; present a consolidated summary focused on material exclusions, exceptions, effective settings and the proposal. Routine row-by-row review, JSON authoring, replacement-list curation or parameter entry is not a normal prerequisite. Preserve explicit benchmark selection and worksheet save, and request additional input only for consequential facts that cannot be safely derived or preset, such as missing sale basis or unresolved supplied account facts. Basic proposal defaults must be documented actual inputs with finite enabled economic limits; presetting does not permit hidden borrowing, relaxed caps, guessed facts or automatic execution. Current implementation readiness remains in STATUS; this paragraph is the adopted target flow.

## User and job

The first user already owns a supported individual-stock/direct-indexing sleeve, understands a benchmark/risk budget and can place specific-lot trades manually. Prosper estimates harvest opportunities and resulting sleeve risk; the user owns financial settings, account activity and broker execution. The project also serves a FinTech learning/showcase goal, without making experiments prerequisites to a useful application.

V1 supports U.S. federal taxable investing, ordinary domestic-corporation common equity, USD, one traded account and relevant supplied household restrictions. Unsupported tax classifications/events or ambiguous identity remain visible and block affected suggestions. ETF units, bonds, derivatives, cash-only onboarding and appreciated-ETF transitions are outside the initial modeled portfolio. Full source observations remain available for diagnostics/restrictions.

Security mapping uses best-effort attributable evidence. Keep accurately mapped supported cash equities; exclude unsupported instruments, securities that cannot be accurately mapped and names without adequate required market history from the modeled universe. A stable identifier is not a universal prerequisite, and routine supported mappings/exclusions do not require repeated owner decisions. Preserve all original rows, explicit exclusion reasons and known/unknown excluded values, and show the modeled coverage. Highlight excluded exposure strictly above **10 bps in one name** or **100 bps in aggregate**, evaluated separately for the original pre-exclusion portfolio/benchmark; unknown coverage stays explicit. These are warnings, not automatic stops. Cash equity means the underlying individual stock, not cash or a cash equivalent. The [market-input policy](../domain/BENCHMARK_INPUTS.md#history-exclusions-and-coverage-highlights) governs evidence/coverage and EOD dates; retained names still need complete required inputs.

For an unmapped personal holding, ordinary selected-snapshot review offers **Mark as Cash**, **Mark as Cash equivalent** and **Undo cash treatment**, including money-market/sweep funds that need no equity-identifier lookup. Show the position's reported name/value beside the control and allow the client to change or undo the treatment. Save it as an explicit user classification bound to the original source/account/date/holding, preserving source identity/classification/value and displaying the effective treatment. It resolves the equity-mapping exception and places the position in the cash/cash-equivalent view rather than requiring an equity identity/history or making it an eligible stock trade. Keep the position in source/coverage accounting and original findings/restrictions visible. Use supported supplied amount/currency/date facts; if missing or contradictory, show unknown rather than inventing zero or reconstructing a balance. Matching lot and independent-position values count once. Report cash/cash-equivalent value separately from the editable amount selected for investment. Classification alone does not assert available buying power, automatically redeem a fund or add its value to the spending budget. A change creates a new selected immutable snapshot revision and invalidates current results; historical results remain unchanged. A fresh import does not silently inherit treatment. Known mapped ordinary equities are outside this cash-control scope.

## Investor workflow

1. **Import current holdings/lots.** Preserve original bytes, account/security/lot references, remaining decimal quantity, reported total adjusted basis, as-of/import dates and available term/classification facts. Durable broker lot IDs, separate positions/cash and complete transaction history are useful optional evidence, not universal prerequisites. File-local row IDs do not claim stable identity across downloads. If independent positions exist, compare them; contradictions are material. Never replay optional activity into holdings already represented by the snapshot.
2. **Resolve scoped facts.** Populate attributable account/security/date facts and supported mappings from the source/rules, preset routine settings and surface only unresolved consequential facts/restrictions for user input. Let the client mark an unmapped position Cash or Cash equivalent directly in ordinary review, preserving source facts and the user treatment. Apply explicit unsupported/unmapped/inadequate-history exclusions with complete source accounting; user-marked cash is displayed as cash treatment rather than a remaining equity-mapping failure. Source-reported basis is a disclosed ordinary-case opening assumption, not a reconstructed tax ledger. Missing basis blocks that lot's loss/sale estimate; unknown term cannot support ST/LT or tax-dollar claims. Keep other holdings/diagnostics useful.
3. **Review screening and cash.** Missing history permits conditional results with a persistent unverified-wash-sale warning. Optional names-only restrictions exclude both sale and replacement buys; known imported conflicts cannot be cleared by omission. Display optional imported cash separately from the editable additional-cash budget, initially visible zero when unset. Positive manual funding is an assumption, not verified buying power. The optional final cash target is separate, explicitly enabled and constrained. No hidden investor thresholds or automatic use of all account cash.
4. **Select benchmark and market inputs.** BlackRock iShares is the default provider; explicitly choose ITOT (U.S. total market) or IVV (S&P 500), with a visible latest-reported date choice or advanced specific full-portfolio date. Vanguard VTI/VOO remains optional. Use the standard risk preset and supported benchmark stocks as the default replacement pool. Review a consolidated equity-only proxy/source/exclusion/settings summary and unresolved exceptions, with advanced controls optional. No default fund, basket, neighboring-date or source fallback; existing saved selections remain explicit. Later-date use requires covered total-return weight drift. Normal marks are EOD; manual history refresh reuses compatible data. Marks, history and classification remain distinct inputs for their metrics.
5. **Inspect baseline and opportunities.** Display estimated eligible lot losses, unsupported/unknown reasons, modeled sleeve value, baseline risk/exposure breaches and optional scenario inputs. The modeled sleeve is retained equities plus selected cash; untouched cash/excluded holdings do not enter its risk denominator. Missing required risk blocks validated risk/candidates, not the holdings view. Scope warnings are carried into saved/exported results.
6. **Compare suggestions.** Show no-trade plus a bounded set of independently checked whole-share candidates with estimated loss, costs, cash, TE/group risk, constraints, data dates and search limits. Strict caps, zero positive gains and known-conflict exclusions are not relaxed automatically. A limited search finding no candidate is not a proof of mathematical infeasibility.
7. **Save/export a dated worksheet.** Save immutable inputs/settings/results and export unambiguous lot/security references, sides/whole shares, indicative prices/costs, assumptions and warnings. Editable settings produce a new checked result. A saved worksheet is an advisory estimate, with no broker order, reservation, fill, funding or deductible-loss guarantee. The user verifies current broker lot selection/funding before manual trades.
8. **Return with refreshed holdings.** Reopen current-format historical output without recalculation; create a new analysis from new imports/settings/data. After external trading or material changes, refresh holdings before another suggestion. Current-format backup/restore protects saved work; development-breaking schema changes need no compatibility/migration support.

## Readiness and failure behavior

After selecting an original source-shaped holdings snapshot and a same-date supported IVV benchmark, **Prepare stock mappings** stages local identities and a complete exclusion summary without a manual identity file. Tickers preserve share-class punctuation; names use documented case/whitespace/punctuation comparison, with no fuzzy match or guessed alias. Existing supplied identities are preserved. Ambiguous, unsupported or unmatched holdings stay in source accounting with explicit stock exclusions, known/unknown values and strict 10/100 bps coverage warnings. User cash treatment stays separate and unchanged. Optional reasoned exclusions are under Advanced controls; internal IDs and locators are in collapsed evidence. Review and selection are explicit, and a newer source/benchmark/cash choice cannot be overwritten by an old draft. Preparation is confined to the source date and supported layout; it does not establish tax classification, history continuity or financial acceptance. Missing required facts still block their dependent estimates.

Before benchmark mapping or portfolio import, inspect saved source observations as readable rows with source/date facts, section counts and grouped located issues. Section/text/issue filters and pagination preserve every retained row and exact supplied value. Distinguish missing facts, empty filters and failed/pending observations. This source inspection does not review identities, reconcile economic holdings or select a benchmark; the explicit reviewed selection remains separate.

Prepare benchmark metadata, section/row decisions and attributable evidence directly in the local form, without authoring a JSON document. The application binds retained source/row identities mechanically and prefills inspected IVV section/row mappings and policy exclusions, showing original-source values/coverage/materiality warnings. **Prepare official fund metadata** checks the fixed official IVV page's fund/date facts and retains its complete original up to 4 MiB. It fills attributable identity/evidence and zero modeled cash by the equity-only proxy definition; modeled cash is distinct from fund cash and investor buying power. Checked evidence is cached and reused on reopen without a new download. Automatic checks never assert that the user reviewed metadata. Manual file/identity/evidence overrides stay under collapsed **Advanced fund metadata**; wrong/unavailable evidence has an explicit error/retry with no alternate fund/date fallback.

Other layouts and unavailable financial facts remain unresolved. History/tax status is explicitly unassessed; local symbol keys do not imply external provider aliases. Paged editing and explicit selected-row evidence/exclusion actions preserve the full ledger. The source-preparation summary stays distinct from the financial preview of edited decisions. Preview exposes missing/stale/contradictory facts through the same current review checks; editing invalidates a prior preview, and metadata changes require renewed preparation or manual confirmation. Saving and selecting remain explicit. Unsaved drafts clear on reload/source change; retained checked metadata and saved results use current backup/reopen behavior. A prepared review file remains optional.

Undated manual restricted names do not automatically expire into a clean result; reconfirm them on later runs. User omissions cannot clear actual known conflicts or invent complete household/history visibility.

Track input completeness, tax/screening scope, market/model quality, candidate validation and freshness separately. A generic warning cannot override missing basis/identity, a known conflict, contradictory included facts, invalid required risk, unavailable shares or inadequate funding. Unknown is not zero. Exclusions cannot erase a known household conflict or unbounded effect on included holdings.

Staging is separate from transactional selection/replacement. Failed/repeated imports preserve selected work; duplicate UI retries cannot duplicate mutations. Late calculations cannot overwrite newer relevant input/settings. One bounded calculation/writer policy and visible running/failure/cancel status suffice; a background orchestration platform is not required. Imported text is data, including formulas/HTML-looking content. Preserve private local handling and safe worksheet export.

## Boundaries and acceptance

Execution/account reconstruction, basis maintenance, order/fill tracking, reservations, forward monitoring and background validation scheduling are deferred. Future trading can change tax outcomes; v1 reports that limitation rather than operating a monitoring service. Cross-year facts remain relevant to screening even without such a service.

The adopted objective and hard checks are in [PORTFOLIO_CONSTRUCTION](../domain/PORTFOLIO_CONSTRUCTION.md), tax policy in [TAX_POLICY](../domain/TAX_POLICY.md), market inputs in [BENCHMARK_INPUTS](../domain/BENCHMARK_INPUTS.md), and the selected method in [RISK_MODEL](../domain/RISK_MODEL.md). [VALIDATION](../engineering/VALIDATION.md) separates local software evidence from independent review, paper acceptance, real-use and public release. Windows x64 is the first investor platform; supported versions/install/scale require observed evidence. No account access, real portfolio disclosure, trade placement or publication is authorized by this specification.
